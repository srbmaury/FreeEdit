# The Moon Is Drifting Away

## Sources

- Apollo 8 Earthrise photograph: NASA / Bill Anders, taken December 24, 1968. Used as a slow-push opening shot. [NASA image page](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/apollo-8s-iconic-earthrise/)
- Earth–Moon orbital visualization: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio. [NASA media page](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/earth-and-moon/)
- Fact check: lunar laser ranging finds the Moon's distance increasing by 3.8 cm per year. Earth's tidal bulge pulls the Moon forward as Earth rotates. [NASA/JPL](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/the-apollo-experiment-that-keeps-on-giving/)
